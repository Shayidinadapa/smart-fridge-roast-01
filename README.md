A one-line roast of a smart fridge, written for the [Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9) bounty on make.fun.

## The roast

> My fridge beeps 'door open' at me like I broke in — buddy, I pay the electric bill.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the roast — a single line of at most 160 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. It runs on Node 22 with no dependencies and no network access.
